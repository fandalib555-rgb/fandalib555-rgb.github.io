About me

Hi, I'm Farhan.

I'm studying a Bachelor of Psychology and Sociology at the University of Melbourne. I've been tutoring since 2020, both privately and with tutoring companies including HZ Tutoring, JDN Tuition, Apex Tuition and TutorDoctor.
